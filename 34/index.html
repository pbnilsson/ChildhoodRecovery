About

Exercises for the recovery of childhood was a series of Facebook posts composed by Andy Stallings, poet, teacher, father, friend. They were gifted to educator and musician Peter Nilsson after a yard sale in which Peter sold Andy his stereo system at an absurdly low price. They were prepared for publication by two generous collaborators: typeface designer David Jonathan Ross and designer James Lindeman. And they were recently read by you, dear reader. We hope they brought you some joy!
